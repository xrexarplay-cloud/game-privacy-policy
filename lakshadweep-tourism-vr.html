Privacy Policy

Lakshadweep Tourism VR — Meta Quest

Exarplay / XR Horizon Pvt. Ltd.

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Overview. Lakshadweep Tourism VR is an immersive educational and tourism experience featuring an introductory history video, an interactive island-selection map, and 360-degree videos of Lakshadweep islands. This policy describes privacy practices for that experience.

1. Information you provide

The app, as currently designed, does not require you to create a separate account or enter your name, email address, phone number, or postal address in order to view the experience.

2. Headset and interaction data

The Meta Quest headset supplies motion and controller input so that you can look around, navigate the island map, and control video playback. These inputs are used to operate the VR experience. The app is not designed to use eye-tracking, microphone recordings, or camera capture for this experience.

3. Videos and local storage

Video content is displayed when you start the introduction or select an island. The app may use temporary files, caches, or local settings needed for media playback and operation. Data stored locally is generally removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to the device's operating-system behavior.

4. Internet connection and third parties

Some media may be delivered from online hosting rather than included in the installation. If online content is used, the hosting service may process technical connection information (for example, IP address and access logs) to deliver media. Meta independently processes information associated with the headset, app distribution, purchases where applicable, and platform services under its own privacy terms. Refer to Meta's Privacy Center for more information.

5. Why information is used

  • To display immersive 360-degree videos and historical content.
  • To respond to island selections and VR controller input.
  • To maintain app functionality and, where applicable, deliver online media.
  • To respond if you contact us for support.

6. Sharing and advertising

We do not sell personal information collected through this experience. The described app does not include user-to-user messaging or a user-profile feature. Any external hosting or platform providers involved in delivering the app may handle technical data according to their own policies.

7. Retention and deletion

For locally stored app data, you can remove the app from your headset to delete its locally managed files, subject to Meta Quest system behavior. If you have sent us a support request, you may contact us to request access to or deletion of personal information we hold in relation to that request. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the described in-app experience. Access and age eligibility are also subject to Meta Quest account requirements and the rating assigned to the app. If a parent or guardian has a concern, please contact us.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by us. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if app functionality, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
