1. Introduction

Exarplay is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our VR applications and Mobile games handle your information while using Meta Quest devices and Mobile devices.

2. Information We Collect

Our applications do not collect personal data such as your name, email address, or phone number directly. However, Meta Platform Features may process limited user data required for gameplay and platform functionality.

  • 👤 User ID: Used to uniquely identify players and save game-related progress and purchases

This data is accessed only as necessary for core application features and is not used for advertising, tracking, or analytics.

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Since only limited platform-provided data is used and no sensitive personal data is stored, risks are minimized.

Our applications integrate with Meta (Oculus) platform services to enable core functionality such as user identification and in-app purchases. No additional third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks are used.
